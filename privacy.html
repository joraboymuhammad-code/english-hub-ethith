1. Overview (English)

ETHITH Company (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the English house app by ETHITH mobile application and web platform (english-hub-ethith). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and how users can request deletion of their account and personal data.

2. Information We Collect

To provide interactive English learning, attendance tracking, homework grading, and classroom communication, we collect only the minimum data required:

  • Account Information: Full name, assigned login username, encrypted/protected login password, role (student, teacher, or administrator), and parent or student phone number (provided by the learning center administrator or teacher during enrollment).
  • Educational & App Activity: Interactive workbook answers, unit test and level test scores, attendance records, homework submissions, learning rewards (in-app coins), and classroom chat messages sent within assigned study groups.
  • User-Uploaded Content: Images or files voluntarily selected by the user via the Android system file picker when submitting assignments or test materials.

3. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate registered students, teachers, and center administrators into their assigned classes.
  • Save workbook answers, grades, attendance records, and homework progress across devices.
  • Enable communication between teachers, students, and learning center administrators.
  • Maintain platform security, performance, and technical support.
No Ads & No Data Selling: English house app by ETHITH does not display third-party advertisements, does not collect or use the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), and does not sell or rent personal user data to third parties.

4. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

All data transmitted between the app and our cloud infrastructure (GitHub / Firebase Realtime Database) is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access to student, class, and administrative data is restricted by role-based authentication.

5. Children’s & Students’ Privacy

Our platform is used by English learning centers serving students aged 13 and older as well as adult learners. Student accounts are created and managed by authorized teachers or administrators with parental/guardian awareness. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verified parental or school authorization. Parents or guardians may review or request deletion of a student’s data at any time.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and educational progress data only for as long as the student or teacher remains active at the learning center, or until a deletion request is submitted.

Account & Data Deletion Request (Hisob va ma’lumotlarni o‘chirish)

Users, parents, or legal guardians can request permanent deletion of their English house app by ETHITH account and all associated personal data at any time without needing to log into the app:

  • Email Request: Send an email to joraboymuhammad@gmail.com with the subject line Account Deletion Request - English House by ETHITH and include your full name and login username.
  • Phone / Center Request: Call or message our support line at +998 95 007 96 21 or contact your English House center administrator directly.

What is deleted: Your profile (name, username, password, phone number), class assignment, workbook responses, chat messages, and coin balance are permanently erased from our cloud database within 7 business days of verification. No personal data is retained after deletion except where required by applicable law.
